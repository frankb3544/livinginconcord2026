# Design principles

Modern Authority: A refined, professional aesthetic that establishes immediate trust and local expertise. The design balances corporate reliability with approachable, community-focused warmth.
- Color Strategy: Deep Navy anchors the experience (trust, foundation, authority), while vibrant Green drives action and draws the eye to key conversion points (growth, vitality, real estate).
- Typography: Replaced the requested generic 'Inter' with 'Outfit' for bold, geometric headings that command attention, paired with 'Plus Jakarta Sans' for highly legible, modern body copy. This creates a distinctive, premium feel.
- Layout & Elevation: Clean, card-based architecture with soft, diffused shadows. Surfaces are distinctly separated from the slightly off-white background to create depth and readability without clutter.
- Shapes: Moderate border radii (12px) on cards and buttons to soften the interface, making the high-authority content feel accessible and human.
- Inspiration: High-end modern brokerages (e.g., Compass, Redfin premium tiers) mixed with editorial city guides.
